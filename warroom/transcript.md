### Recommendation

**PRIMARY: The Forgetting Gallery** — Highest score with verified claims. Targets the EMPTY Artistic Excellence track. The memory-wipe mechanic is genuinely novel (CROSS: Gaming → Art Scarcity). Demo is visually striking in 90 seconds: an AI agent creates art, then cryptographically forgets how to make it again — each piece is provably one-of-a-kind. Lower build risk than Genesis Canvas (no real-time battles, no audience betting infrastructure). Clean story: "What if AI art could only be created ONCE?"

**BACKUP: Alter Ego** — Lowest build risk. Chat-based demo is reliable. Targets LOW-density Lifestyle track + Social Buzz crossover. Two Alter Egos roasting each other with real on-chain transaction history = maximum theater in 90 seconds. Natural viral X post material (#OKXAI). Revenue potential: users pay to create/maintain their Alter Ego.

**STRETCH: Genesis Canvas** — Highest ambition, highest risk. The TEE art battle is the most impressive demo IF it works, but real-time image generation + TEE attestation + betting escrow in 2 days is a tight squeeze. Only choose this if you're confident in the image generation pipeline.
