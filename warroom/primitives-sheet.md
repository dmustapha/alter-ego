## Winning Patterns (from catalog)
1. **One novel mechanic, deeply explored** — not breadth
2. **Demo IS the product** — live experiences, not architecture diagrams
3. **Chain-native, not chain-agnostic** — remove the chain, product breaks
4. **Live, running, producing value** — "is doing" beats "could do"
5. **AI + crypto convergence** — autonomous decisions with on-chain accountability
6. **Privacy/Security as core mechanic** — not additive

## Anti-Patterns (what LOST)
1. Infrastructure that enables vs. experiences that do
2. Breadth of integrations, depth in none
3. Testnet token economics as innovation
4. Bottom-up building instead of demo-first
5. "Chat with your wallet" clones
6. Generic price bots / data dashboards
