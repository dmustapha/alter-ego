Hook

Every wallet has a story. Alter Ego reveals the trader you become on each chain — then stages a roast battle between your personas, backed by real transaction data.

OKX.AI Genesis Hackathon · Lifestyle Companion + Social Buzz

The Problem

Crypto traders manage multiple wallets across multiple chains. On Ethereum, they might be disciplined DCA investors. On Solana, they could be high-frequency meme-coin degen. No tool surfaces this identity split — you see separate wallets, not the full picture of who you are as a trader. The same person running smart exits on ETH is panic-selling on SOL, and they do not know it.

Existing portfolio trackers show balances. They do not tell you why you trade the way you do, or what it costs you.

The Solution

Alter Ego is a TEE-bound agent that ingests your complete on-chain history across every wallet and chain. It classifies every trade, builds a psychological profile, assigns archetypes (The Professional, The Degen), then pits your Ethereum self against your Solana self in a 5-round roast battle backed by real transaction data. The output is a cryptographic snapshot you can share or sell.

The core insight: self-knowledge is the product. You are not one trader. You are multiple traders. Alter Ego makes you look at all of them, side by side, and shows you the GAP COST: what you lost by not being your best self on every chain.

How It Works

  1. Paste any wallet address. EVM or Solana. Alter Ego pulls complete portfolio data and transaction history through the OnchainOS CLI layer (OKX WALLET + DEX-MARKET integrations).
  2. Pattern classification engine. Every trade gets tagged as AMPLIFY (strength: Diamond Hands, Smart Exits) or GUARD (risk: Panic Selling, Meme Chasing) with confidence scores.
  3. Persona builder assigns archetypes. The Professional. The Degen. The Whale. Each persona gets a superpower, a kryptonite, and a win/loss rate.
  4. 5-round roast battle. Your Ethereum persona and Solana persona face off. Every insult is backed by real transaction data. "You held through a 45% drawdown — that is not conviction, that is a coma." GUARD tags flash pink when targeted.
  5. Compare card reveals the cost. Side-by-side PnL, win rates, and the GAP COST — $31,500 in the demo wallet — what you lost by not being your best self on every chain.
  6. Cryptographic snapshot via x402. TEE-sealed attestation pinned to the agent identity. Pay $0.99 via x402 (USDC on Base) to lock a permanent, shareable proof.

Key Features

  • Multi-chain behavioral fingerprinting: Ethereum, Solana, X Layer. 4,463 transactions analyzed across the demo wallets.
  • Persona archetype engine: assigns trading personalities based on real patterns, not heuristics.
  • 5-round roast battle: each round fires a data-backed insult at the opposing persona. Pink/cyan/yellow speaker colors, CRT scanline effects.
  • GAP COST calculation: quantifies the real dollar difference between your best and worst trading persona.
  • TEE-sealed attestation: cryptographic proof pinned to the agent identity. Verifiable, private, shareable.
  • x402 micropayment gate: 3-state transition (idle → simulating → done). Snapshot at $0.99, coaching subscription at $4.99/mo.
